Source: Haoyu Hu, FloodPred dissertation, PDF physical pages 9 and 13 (sections 3.4 and 4.1).

The same evaluation is repeated across ten randomly selected one-year windows. Each window contains 35,040 consecutive observations, corresponding to one year of 15-minute data. A fixed random seed of 2026 makes the window selection reproducible.
Since the complete dataset covers less than three years, some selected windows overlap. The experiment is therefore used to examine performance across different temporal sections of the dataset rather than as ten independent annual evaluations.

Across the ten year-long event evaluations, it detected an average of 15.0 out of 17.4 recorded flood events per run. It missed 2.4 events and produced 3.7 false alarms, giving a mean recall of 86.19% and a mean precision of 81.50%.
The mean peak error was -0.07 m, indicating slight underprediction, while warnings were generated an average of 16.2 hours before the observed peak.
