Source: Haoyu Hu, FloodPred dissertation, PDF physical page 10 (section 3.5).

A forecast is classified as “Caution”, the lowest alert above No risk, when its maximum level reaches 4.20 m.
It is classified as “Warning” when it reaches 4.43 m or remains above this level for at least one hour.
“Severe” is issued when the predicted maximum reaches 4.70 m, remains above 4.43 m for at least two hours, or is expected to cross 4.43 m within two hours.
