Source: Haoyu Hu, FloodPred dissertation, PDF physical pages 14 and 16 (section 4.2).

The deployed system successfully recorded 817 forecast runs and 78,432 forecast points.
Points without complete future observations were excluded, leaving 76,091 forecast-observation pairs with 100% matching coverage.
The model achieved an MAE of 0.213 m

During the evaluated period, no observed level reached the 4.20 m Caution threshold. The highest actual and predicted levels were 3.907 m and 4.082 m. All 769 completed risk evaluations therefore remained at No risk, with no false alarms. This confirms stable behaviour during a non-flood period but cannot demonstrate live flood detection because no observed event crossed the threshold.
