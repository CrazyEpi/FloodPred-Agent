# House Mill: heritage context (curated paraphrase)
Historic England lists the House Mill at Three Mills, Bromley-by-Bow, London as a Grade I listed tide mill, built in 1776 (list entry 1080970). This describes the historic building, not a current flood status.
Source: https://historicengland.org.uk/listing/the-list/list-entry/1080970 ; checked 2026-09-30.
