# Why water at House Mill matters (curated paraphrase)
Wilson and Zhang, Uncharted Waters (2025), PDF pp. 1-2: the House Mill is a historic timber tidal mill on the River Lea. River levels and incoming Thames tides can bring water into contact with its beams and floorboards. Short or out-of-hours events may be missed by volunteers. These are study observations, not today's conditions.
